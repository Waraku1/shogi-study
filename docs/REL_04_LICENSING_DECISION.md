## Mandatory conditions versus optional assurance

Applicable license grants, notices, complete corresponding preferred source and valid delivery/access conditions are compliance matters. **Independent rebuilding and byte-for-byte reproducibility are optional assurance, not automatic GPL conditions or license approval.** The prior independent build did not reach compilation; no replacement engine is built here. Absence of a byte-identical rebuild does not automatically prove GPL noncompliance; a matching hash would not automatically prove source completeness either. The owner must choose the assurance level and resolve any concrete source deficiency before distribution.

Primary references already used in REL-02: [GPLv3 §§0,1,6](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html), [FSF source-delivery FAQ](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.html#SourceAndBinaryOnDifferentSites), retained component notices and pinned font OFL. Record decisions without secrets; until explicitly approved, leave all blocking metadata and root LICENSE unchanged.
